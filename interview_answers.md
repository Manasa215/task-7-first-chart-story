# Task 7 — Interview Questions

## 1. When would you choose a bar chart over a line chart?
I choose a bar chart when I need to compare values across separate categories, such as sales by product or revenue by region. I choose a line chart when I want to show a trend or change over a continuous period, such as monthly sales.

## 2. Why are pie charts often discouraged in professional reporting?
Pie charts can be difficult to compare when slices are similar in size, and too many slices make them cluttered. A bar chart is often clearer for precise comparisons. Pie charts are most useful when showing a simple part-to-whole relationship with only a few categories.
